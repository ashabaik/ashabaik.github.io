01 — Start here

Two paths. Pick the one that sounds like you.

Nothing gets built until we both know what it is for.

AI have an idea

Let's get your idea out of your head and online.

For you if you want to start selling online, offer a new service in your city, or test an idea with real customers before you build the whole thing.

  1. Say it out loud

    You tell me the idea in plain words: what it is, who will pay for it, and why they'd pick you.

  2. Choose the first version

    We decide the few things it must do on day one, and what can wait until people are using it.

  3. I design and build it

    I design and build that first version, and you follow it on a live preview link until it is ready to launch.

BI have a business

Keep what works. Fix what slows you down.

For you if the business runs, but too much of it runs by hand: a shop taking orders in chat, a clinic booking by phone, a company living in spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups.

  1. Walk me through a normal day

    You show me how orders, bookings, staff, and numbers move through your business right now.

  2. Find where the time goes

    We look for the spots where a website, a system, or some automation would save time or bring in customers.

  3. Build the fix that matters most

    I build what we agreed on, no more than you need, and you follow it on a live preview link.

02 — How I work

What happens after you message me.

Before any code, I ask

  • Who buys from you, and why?
  • How do orders reach you today?
  • Where does your team lose time?
  • What must work on day one?
  • What can wait until later?

Then we decide what to build.

  1. 01

    I learn your business

    I ask what you sell, who buys it, and what you want to change. No technology talk yet.

  2. 02

    We agree on what to build

    We choose the smallest thing that does the job. What I'll build, the price, and the date are agreed before I start.

  3. 03

    I design and build

    You get a live preview link early, so you can watch it come together instead of waiting for a big reveal.

  4. 04

    You go live

    I test on desktop and mobile before delivery. You get the files, and my help after launch.

03 — Services

Three ways I help a business grow.

01

Websites & web apps

A site or web app that tells customers what you offer and makes the next step easy, whether that's a call, a booking, or an order.

  • Business and personal websites
  • Landing pages for a launch or a single offer
  • Web apps with an admin area, so you update things yourself
  • Arabic and English, with proper right-to-left

Under the hood: HTML · CSS · JavaScript

02

Data & automation

Your numbers land in one place and stay up to date, instead of someone copying them from sheet to sheet by hand.

  • Data pulled from your tools automatically
  • Tables set up ready for your reports
  • Your apps connected, so they pass information to each other

Under the hood: ETL / ELT pipelines · data warehouses · API integrations · Microsoft Fabric · AWS · GCP · Spark

03

Social media

Pages that stay active and sound like you, so customers see a business that is open and paying attention.

  • Your pages managed for you
  • Posts designed and written
  • Short videos
  • Paid ads

Under the hood: Made with AI tools. Everything is reviewed before it is posted.
